## Changes

- iOS Master output is reduced by 14 dB, covering both music layers and all SFX.
  This is about 20% of the previous signal amplitude; perceived loudness must
  still be checked on an iPhone/iPad at the lowest hardware volume step.
  Other platforms retain their current output level.
- Stage selection shows three equal cards per page. Touch/mouse swipes,
  entitlement locks and returning to the selected stage's page are preserved.
  An incomplete last page keeps the same card widths.
- 1-9 retains its six acts, 18 machines and 89 enemies, with six bilingual route
  boards, curtains, bunting, footlights and a finish arch. New upper routes use
  two moving platforms and two crumbling steps, with permanent recovery ledges.
  Eighteen coin trails now contain 72 coins. The final checkpoint is on the
  lower balcony, below the waiting crowd.
- The mouth bridge warns before its lethal bite; fireworks signal their launch
  beat. The cannon shows a short preview using its actual launch velocity.
  Collision, telegraphs and machinery continue to use the shared Clock.
- Stage dressing is one static mesh per act, avoiding individual draw calls
  for each bulb and pennant. Stage handshake enum values are unchanged.
